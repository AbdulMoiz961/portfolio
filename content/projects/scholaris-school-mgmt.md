A full-stack academic management system structured as an npm workspace monorepo. Implements role-aware workflows for administrators, teachers, and students covering attendance, timetable scheduling with interval conflict detection, grading, fee tracking, and an immutable field-level audit trail. Built with strict TypeScript end-to-end, shared domain contracts, and business rules enforced at the service and database layer.
