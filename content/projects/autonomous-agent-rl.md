A reinforcement learning project training an autonomous agent to execute stable landings in Gymnasium's LunarLander-v3 environment. Implemented continuous policy optimization using Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO), navigating an 8-dimensional continuous state space and discrete thruster controls to surpass the environment's benchmark score of +200 episodic reward.
